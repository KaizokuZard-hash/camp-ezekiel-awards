The Categories

Vote for the region whose camp deserves it. You get one vote in each category — choose carefully, it locks in once you submit.

One rule: vote honest

Everybody turns up for their own region. That's the easy vote — and if everyone casts it, these awards just measure who brought the most people, which is a much more boring competition.

The vote worth casting is for the camp that made you sit up even though it wasn't yours. Nobody sees your ballot — no name, no login, nothing that traces back to you. There is nobody to impress and nothing to defend, so pick what actually deserves it.

That's why your own region is greyed out. You're judging the other four — which is the only way this measures the camps rather than who brought the biggest crowd.
